I'm not huge on rules. However people do exist and common common-sense
is not common. Below is a list of guidelines I feel are a reasonable
expectation of participation. The strict rules are for [the TOS](./tos)

- <b>Be considerate</b>: Words can hurt. Emotions are real. Hold respect for others.

- <b>Respect boundaries</b>: If someone makes it clear they are uncomfortable with something, desist. 

- <b>Be ethical</b>: Sure we do a little trolling. Keep it cute. 
Keep it clean. A harmless joke is funny. Ruining someones life
isn't.

- <b>Keep it above the belt</b>: Don't insult people for things that they
cant change. Be mature and realize that it doesn't kill you to respect
pronouns. 

Blah Blah, muh free speech. This is my space, I run it how I like. If 
you're being a problem, youre not welcome here. Go host your own
server and follow your own rules there. Free speech is
the right to speak freely, not the right to have someone hold a
space for you to be heard.

If you come across anything that violates the Code of Conduct that
we should know about, please let us know by reaching out to [jjj333_p](/jjj333_p).
